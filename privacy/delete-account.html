Account Deletion – Freshcart-Ecom

You can request deletion of your Freshcart-Ecom account and associated data.

Steps

  1. Email us with subject: “Freshcart Account Deletion Request”
  2. Include your registered email/phone number.
  3. We may ask for verification to confirm ownership.

Email: support@yourdomain.com

For deleting only specific data (without deleting your account), see: Delete Data
