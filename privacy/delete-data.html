Data Deletion Request

App: Freshcart-Ecom
Developer: Freshcart Team
Last updated: 21 Dec 2025

Need your account and/or data deleted? Follow the steps below. We will verify the request to protect your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to: support@freshcart-ecom.com
  2. Use this subject: Freshcart-Ecom - Data Deletion Request
  3. In the email, include:
    • Your registered email address / phone number used in the app
    • Your full name (as in the app)
    • Request type: Delete Account or Delete Specific Data
    • If deleting specific data, mention exactly what you want deleted (example: delivery address, order history)

What data we delete

When we process a deletion request, we delete or anonymize the following data associated with your account (as applicable):

  • Account information: name, email, user ID
  • Profile details and saved addresses
  • Order history and cart data
  • App activity data linked to your account (if stored on our servers)

What data we may retain

We may retain certain data if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or tax/compliance purposes. If retained, it will be limited and protected.

Processing timeline

  • Verification: typically within 1–3 business days
  • Deletion completion: typically within 7–30 business days
