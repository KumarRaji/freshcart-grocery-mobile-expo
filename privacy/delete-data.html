Data Deletion Request – Freshcart-Ecom

You can request deletion of specific data associated with your Freshcart-Ecom account (for example: profile details, order history, addresses).

How to request data deletion

  1. Email us with the subject: “Freshcart Data Deletion Request”
  2. Include your registered email/phone number and (optional) your user ID.
  3. Tell us what data you want deleted (e.g., addresses only, order history, or all account data).

What happens next

  • We may verify ownership before processing the request.
  • We will complete deletion within a reasonable time (typically within 7–30 days).
  • Some data may be retained if required by law (tax/invoice compliance), but we will minimize it.
